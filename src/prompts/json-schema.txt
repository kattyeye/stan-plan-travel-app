RECIPE RULES:
- Each cook-in meal in itinerary must have a matching entry in "recipes" with the same "id"
- "recipeId" values in itinerary and groceryList.byMeal must exactly match a recipe "id"
- Scale all ingredient amounts to the exact total group size

RESTAURANT RULES:
- Only include real, currently operating restaurants
- Each restaurant must have a unique "id"

ACTIVITY TIME RULES:
- "title" must never contain a time. Write "Kayak rental at Kitty Hawk", not
  "9:00am — Kayak rental at Kitty Hawk". Times belong in startTime/endTime so
  the plan can be exported to a calendar.
- Times are 24-hour "HH:MM" (e.g. "09:00", "18:30"), local to the destination.
- Activities within a day must be ordered chronologically.
- Do not overlap timed activities on the same day.

PLANNING STYLE RULES:
- structured: every activity has startTime (endTime where the duration is
  known); every meal has a time. Language is direct and specific.
- balanced: most activities have a startTime reflecting the part of day
  (morning ~09:00, afternoon ~13:00, evening ~18:00); meals have times. Offer a
  primary plan plus one alternative in "notes".
- flexible: omit startTime, endTime and meal "time" entirely. Use suggestion
  language, e.g. title "Kayaking — great here if you feel like it". Untimed
  entries are intentional and export as all-day calendar items.
